Recommendations

Hire vs. equipment vs. rebalance, sized from today's shift headcount against physical station counts
1st shift (6:30am-3pm) production headcount today: 6 2nd shift (3pm-11pm) production headcount today: 6 Physical stations shop-wide: 34
Sizing logic: every physical station needs one dedicated operator each shift to hit its full daily equipment capacity (Station Capacity tab). When a station has idle equipment on a shift (more physical units than assigned operator-equivalents) and it's at risk of falling behind, that's a hire or shift-move call. When every available unit already has an operator on both shifts and it's still behind, more staff wouldn't help — that's an equipment call. When one shift is short while the other has spare coverage for the same station, that's a rebalance, not a net-new hire. Stations with more than a handful of small units (e.g. Video's tape decks) are batch-tended, not 1-to-1 — those fall back to the equipment/staffing utilization signal instead of a literal per-unit headcount.
